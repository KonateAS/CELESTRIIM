# CELESTRIIUM brand assets

The wordmark and emblem in this folder are derived from the logo images supplied by the site owner. They are not generated artwork. The JPEG white backgrounds were removed while preserving the original dark strokes; the wordmark was optimized as WebP and the emblem exported as a 512 px transparent PNG.

- `wordmark.webp` is used by the site header, footer, contact page, and administration sidebar.
- `icon.png` is used for browser and Apple home-screen icons.

To update the identity later, replace these two files while keeping their paths and transparent backgrounds. The source images are retained by the owner; no external image is used.
